Welcome to the AI to Robot Matcher

Every Signature AI documented with its best-matching robot body — documented concept pairings, marching to 1,000,000 pairs. Here’s how to use it:

  1. Find the best-fit body. Type plain words like “gentle helper for grandma” in the finder and press Find the best-fit body — or type an exact ID like JAH-PAIR-000123 to jump to that record.
  2. Browse the tabs. Pairs A–Z browses every pair by letter; Robot Bodies shows the 120 original bodies; Mix Lab lets you forge your own pair from any AI + any body + any mission.
  3. Open a pair record. Match score dial, written rationale, best-fit breakdown — plus a shell-aware demo where the AI answers aware of the body it runs in (honestly badged SIMULATION).
  4. Listen and save. Every record offers Read aloud, Copy, Download .txt / .json, and Share.

How to use this site

🔍 Find best-fit body

What: the finder box. Does: matches your plain words against all archived pairs and shows the top 5. How: type e.g. “warehouse lifter” and press Find best-fit body; type an exact JAH-PAIR-000123 to jump straight to it.

🎲 Random pair

What: the dice button beside the finder. Does: opens one real forged pair at random. How: tap it — every tap resolves to a genuine record, never an invented one.

🔗 Pairs A–Z

What: the Pairs A–Z tab. Does: browses every pair by the AI’s first letter, with a live filter (ID, AI, body, mission) and Prev/Next paging. How: tap the tab, pick a letter, or type in the filter. If nothing matches, the page says so plainly.

🦎 Robot Bodies

What: the Robot Bodies tab. Does: the 120 original Signature robot bodies, grouped by class, each with a full spec sheet: dimensions, mass and payload, materials, actuators, sensors, power, compute, mobility, interfaces, operating limits, safety assumptions. How: tap the tab, open a body; Copy / Download / Read aloud on every sheet.

🧪 Mix Lab

What: the Mix Lab tab. Does: pick any AI, any body, any mission and forge your own pair — deterministic, so the same combo always forges the same pair. How: choose, press Forge pair. Custom pairs get a JAH-MIX-XXXXXX ID and are not stored in the archive.

🧬 Pair records

What: a full pair record. Does: match score dial (0–100, concept fit only — never an engineering recommendation), written rationale (capability, temperament, task fit), best-fit breakdown with the score math, match criteria & limitations, and related pairs (same AI or same body). How: tap any pair to open it.

🎤 Shell-aware demo

What: the demo chat on the pair record. Does: the AI answers aware of the body it runs in — replies come from the on-board demo instantly, honestly badged SIMULATION. How: type a question (e.g. “what’s your mission?”) and press Send. The Pair helper answers from the pair’s own record.

🔊 Read aloud, copy, download, share

What: the record panel buttons. Does: Read aloud (tiered speech: device voice, then audio); Copy (record, ID, or URL); Download .txt / .json; Share; Open. How: the buttons on any open record; the floating bar pauses or stops reading.

📖 Trust & methodology

What: the methodology and manifest links. Does: Methodology explains how matching works; Matcher manifest and AI manifest are the authoritative machine-readable counts; schemas documents the record format; llms.txt is the AI-agent summary. How: the links in the footer. If data ever fails to load, the page says which part failed with a Reload index button.
