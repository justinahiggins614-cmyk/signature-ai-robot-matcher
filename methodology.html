Matcher Methodology

The Signature AI Robot Matcher archives documented concept pairings between Signature AIs and Signature robot bodies. This page defines exactly what a pair, a score, and a status mean — and what they do not mean.

What a pair is

Pair = one deterministic record: an identified AI + an identified robot body + a documented mission + a match score + a written rationale + a shell-aware speaking demo. Pair n of 1,000,000 always resolves to the same AI, body, and mission. Same inputs + same matcher version = same pair, same score, same hash.

The match score (0–100)

Formula: 55 + int(25 × shared_tags ÷ union_tags) + pair_factor(0–19), capped at 100.

  • Base 55 — every documented pairing starts here.
  • Capability-tag overlap (up to +25) — shared capability tags between the AI's skills and the robot body's hardware, as a fraction of their union.
  • Deterministic pair factor (0–19) — derived from the pair number; breaks ties reproducibly.

The score measures documented concept fit only.

What the score is NOT

The score is never: engineering feasibility, a safety rating, production readiness, market readiness, certification, or physical compatibility. A high concept-fit score is never approval to build, deploy, or certify a robot. No physical build or testing has been performed on these pairings.

Three independent statuses

Status axisValuesMeaning
Concept matchCONCEPT / DOCUMENTEDThe AI's role and capabilities fit the robot concept on paper.
Technical compatibilityNOT_CALCULATED / DOCUMENTEDInterfaces, power, compute, and dimensions checked against records.
Physical validationNOT_TESTED / PROTOTYPE / TESTED / VALIDATEDA physical build was actually tested. All archive pairs are NOT_TESTED unless stated.

These axes are independent. A high concept score says nothing about the other two.

Robot gear

Every robot body record carries its full hardware gear: form factor, dimensions, mass, payload, materials, actuators, sensors, power system, compute, mobility, interfaces, operating limits, environment, safety assumptions, and supervision requirements. Where data is genuinely absent the record says NOT SPECIFIED — never an invented specification. Every body carries a SHA-256 content hash, a version, and a canonical URL.

AI identity

Every AI in a pair is identified by its canonical Signature AI Phone Book ID (e.g. JAH-AI-DOM-001). The phone book's ai-catalog.json is the single source of truth for AI identity; this archive links to it and never maintains a competing copy. A coherence check asserts the pool never drifts from the canon.

Find vs Generate

Find searches the existing archive and existing records. Generate (the Mix Lab) creates a new deterministic custom pair with its own JAH-MIX-XXXXXX ID and status — it is never presented as an already-archived catalog pair.

Determinism & verification

Pair generation is fully deterministic: re-running the generator produces byte-identical records. Every body and AI record carries a SHA-256 content hash. The pair index carries a manifest-level hash. Build gates fail the release on duplicate IDs, broken references, count mismatches, or hash failures.
